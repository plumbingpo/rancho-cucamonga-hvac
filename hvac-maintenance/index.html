Warning Signs for HVAC Maintenance

Spotting the early warning signs gives you the chance to act before the situation spirals into something far more expensive. Keep an eye out for the following clues that it's time to bring in an expert:

  • You simply have no idea what condition your equipment is actually in
  • A failure last year that seemed to come out of nowhere
  • Utility bills rising even though nothing about your routine changed
  • Nobody has opened up the system in over a year
  • A startup sound that has changed since last year
  • A filter you cannot recall replacing, or a musty odor at blower startup
  • Recently installed equipment whose manufacturer expects maintenance records
  • Cooling or heating that takes noticeably longer to reach the setpoint

Maintenance Tips for HVAC Maintenance

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly heating and cooling problems down the road. Follow these tips from our experienced technicians:

  • Swap the filter every one to three months, the most valuable habit a homeowner can build
  • Leave every supply and return register open, including those in unused rooms
  • Trim plants back from the outdoor unit and give the coil a gentle rinse every spring
  • Flush the condensate line at the start of cooling season before algae blooms in it
  • Book spring and fall visits in the shoulder seasons, before the rush and before the extremes
  • Note new sounds and longer run times; they are data your technician can use
  • Check carbon monoxide alarms monthly in any home with fuel-burning appliances
